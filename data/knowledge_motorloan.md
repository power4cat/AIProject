## What is MOTORLOAN.MY?

MOTORLOAN.MY is an integrated motorcycle e-marketplace platform developed by SHENMA Credit Sdn. Bhd.; a motorcycle financing provider. The platform offers a wide range of motorcycle selection, a comprehensive dealers’ network and the motorcycle financing package, to make the ownership of motorcycles easier.

## How to buy motorcycle using MOTORLOAN.MY?

MOTORLOAN.MY provides 3 easy steps in buying your dream motorcycle and financing package from SHENMA Credit Sdn. Bhd. Step 1 – Select Motorcycle & Dealer. Step 2 - Sign Up the Financing Agreement. Step 3 - Get Your Dream Motorcycle & Ride in Style!​

## What is the requirement for motorcycle financing?

The applicant must be a Malaysian citizen only and aged 21 years old and above. In permanent employment of at least 6 months OR 12 months for self-employed. Minimum document required; Employed Person; Copy of NRIC, Latest Salary Statement. Self-employed Person; Copy of NRIC, Latest 3 Months Bank Statement & Business Registration Document.

## Is SHENMA Motorcycle Financing a Shariah product?

SHENMA Motorcycle Financing is an Islamic financing product whose structure and legal documentation are in accordance with Shariah rules and principles. The product is based on the Shariah principle of Al-Ijarah Thumma al-Bai’ (AITAB).

## Is the motorcycle price in MOTORLOAN.MY fixed?

No, the Recommended Selling Price for each motorcycle model is set by the dealer and may vary depending on the dealer you choose.

## Am I allowed to change dealers once my application is approved?

You cannot change the dealer directly once your application is approved. However, you can still switch to a different dealer by following these steps: Step 1, Cancel your approved application by contacting our Customer Service team via our official WhatsApp channel: +6011‑33011633, Step 2, Resubmit your application with your newly selected dealer.
